How it works

  1. Pick your industry.General industry, construction, maritime, agriculture, or all of them if you are not sure.
  2. Ask in plain language.Fall protection, forklift training, lockout, injury reporting.
  3. Get the answer and the standard.Every answer cites the exact 29 CFR section and opens it on OSHA.gov.
  4. Know where the answer came from.It flags answers drawn from a letter of interpretation or a compliance directive, points to the General Duty Clause when no standard applies, and notes State Plan differences when they matter.

What it is, and what it is not

It is a fast first look.

It is not the standard itself, and it is not legal advice.

Every answer links to the official text so you can verify before you act. When it matters, read the standard and talk to your safety department.

Questions people ask

Is the OSHA Reg Lookup free?

Yes. There is no fee and no email wall. It runs in Claude, so you need a free Claude account to use it.

Is it legal advice?

No. It is a reference that points you to the exact standard. Read the official text before you act on it.

MSHA or OSHA, which one do I use?

Use the MSHA Reg Lookup for mines under 30 CFR. Use this one when the work falls under OSHA. If you are not sure who has jurisdiction, ask your safety department.
