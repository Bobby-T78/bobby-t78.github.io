Why I built it

Somebody on my crew is always asking about a regulation or a safety concern.

Knowing the answer is not the same as citing it.

In this business the section number matters as much as the answer.

So I built a quick way to look up a regulation, ask a question, and get an answer with the section attached.

The story behind it is in The Book in the Glove Box.

How it works

  1. Pick your mine type.It starts on surface metal and nonmetal, Part 56.
  2. Ask in plain language.Guarding, berms, workplace exams, reporting timelines. Ask it the way you would ask your supervisor.
  3. Get the answer and the section.Every answer cites the exact 30 CFR section it comes from.
  4. Check the official text.Tap the citation to open the regulation on eCFR before you act.

What it is, and what it is not

It is a fast first look.

It is not the book, and it is not legal advice.

Every answer links to the official text so you can verify before you act. When it matters, read the section and talk to your safety department.
